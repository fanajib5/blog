# Editorial Style Revision for 2026 Writing

## Goal

Revise the 2026 writing collection to reduce AI-sounding prose, including excessive em dashes, generic transitions, repetitive framing, and formulaic endings. Use the author's 2023 writing as a style reference.

## Scope

- All Indonesian and English article files under `content/writing/2026/`.
- Include published articles and drafts.
- In drafts, preserve placeholders and editorial notes that request future facts or content.

## Editorial approach

Use a targeted style edit rather than a light punctuation pass or a full rewrite. Preserve each article's intent, factual claims, examples, technical explanations, and useful structure. Make the prose sound more like the 2023 references: personal where the source supports it, concrete, conversational, and willing to use specific details or light humor without forcing those traits into every article.

For Indonesian and English versions, edit each language naturally rather than translating sentence-by-sentence. Remove em dashes where they are stylistic filler; retain punctuation and sentence structures that serve a clear purpose. Replace generic claims and stock transitions or calls to action with specific wording grounded in the article, or remove them when they add no value.

## Constraints

- Do not invent personal experiences, facts, results, or supporting detail.
- Do not change front matter, code samples, quoted material, links, or embedded media.
- Preserve technical terms and explanations when they add useful precision.
- Preserve draft placeholders and notes rather than filling them in.
- Avoid imposing a single template or making every article sound alike.

## Validation

- Review changes against the 2023 reference articles for voice and naturalness.
- Search the 2026 collection for remaining em dashes and inspect any retained uses.
- Review revised prose for generic or repeated transitions, conclusions, and calls to action.
- Confirm protected content and draft placeholders remain unchanged.
